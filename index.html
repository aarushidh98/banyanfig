About Banyan Fig

An Indian design studio weaving architecture, interiors and landscape into a single, emotive narrative – where every surface, shadow and silence has been considered.

Banyan Fig is a design-first architectural and interior studio rooted in Indian light, materiality and craft. We approach every project as a story – editing, layering and refining until the space feels inevitable.

From ground-up architecture to intimate interior interventions, we choreograph proportion, texture and natural light to create homes and spaces that are calm, contemporary and deeply personal.
